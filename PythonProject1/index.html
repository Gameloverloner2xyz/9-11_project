Cheers

By: Me

Cheers to the brave ones

Who had a goal in mind

To save those who can't themselves


Cheers to you


Cheers to those who had family there

Friends

and Distant Relatives

You could never have guessed

That when they walked out the door

They'd never return.


Cheers to you


Cheers to those with integrity

Who do something in honor

With no reward in mind


Cheers to you


And Cheers to those who are here

Remembering the countless lives

Innocent Human Beings

Who died in this tragedy

And the firefighters

And the first responders

And the police officers

Who were courageous that day

In ways words can't describe


Cheers to You
