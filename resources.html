Resources and Where to Help

This page is to provide information on where or what you can do to help. Whether it's helping survivors or veterans or spreading awareness, you can do your part. Mainly in New York but I'll have a couple ideas at the bottom listed if you want ways to help out.

Where to Donate or Volunteer!

You can donate supplies for homeless veterans at the Beacon House!

Participate in the Tunnels To Towers 5k Run

Make weekly calls to lonely veterans

Donate to Tuesday's Promise

Donate to the Wounded Warrior Project!

Volunteer at Rebuilding Together Long Island

Donate at New Ground, Leviitown, NY!

Donate food, goods, or money to the Long Island Cares Veterans Project.

Schedule a home pickup or drop off clothing to Vietnam Veterans of America drop-off spots!

Donate to H.E.A.R.T 9/11



Now I know not everyone here can do these things. You might be a minor, have nothing to donate, or might not live in New York. In that case, here are some things you can do if you fall under one, or all of these.

You can volunteer in your area by writing letters to veterans. Places like a local public library will let you write letters to veterans for community service hours!



If you are a minor, you can still participate in the Tunnels to Towers 5k Run! In fact, since you are a minor you don't have to pay.



Spread Awareness! Make a slideshow presentation or anything about someone you know personally or have researched before that was in 9/11 and demonstrated courage. This is a great way to not let their act remain unseen.



On the topic of spreading awareness, you can make anti-bullying, anti-racism, or anti-discrimination (or anything else along those lines) posters. I had mentioned in the history section that discrimination and hate crimes were a huge problem for muslims after 9/11. Along with that racism is still a problem many face today! Make sure to get permission before hanging up these posters.



You can fundraise and donate if you have no money to donate. You can make a simple lemonade or craft stand (bonus points if the crafts relate to veterans) and sell them to raise a certain amount of money to donate to a trustworthy organization or non-profit.
